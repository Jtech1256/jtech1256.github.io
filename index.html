My framework

When my second PC build stalled on a blank screen, I almost gave up and scheduled a repair. Instead, I went back piece by piece, guided by my parents' stories of starting over in a new country and their reminder that effort matters even when the outcome is uncertain.

That same approach shows up when I coach, debug, or design systems: break problems into smaller parts, stay patient, and keep the people you're serving at the center.

Long-term, I want to design AI systems that are secure, fair, and aligned with communities who are often left out of the design process—especially in areas like healthcare and cybersecurity.
