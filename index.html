I'm a first-year at Virginia Tech from Chantilly, Virginia, studying Computational Modeling and Data Analytics.

My first job in threat detection was a lifeguard chair. You watch the same water for hours and the whole job is catching the one thing that's off before it turns into a problem. Security is the same habit, with more logs and less sunscreen.

Where this is going: data science and cybersecurity for national defense, as a civilian.

Final paper · Calculus I and CMDA · Nov 2026

How the Foundations of Calculus 1 Teaches Computers to Learn

Minimizing a function is one of the first things Calc 1 teaches, and it turns out to be how machine learning models are trained. I walk through it with one small problem: predicting how much battery a delivery drone burns per kilometer from four test flights.

3.41%
battery per km, solved two ways
318 → 66
loss after one gradient step
α < 0.037
where the learning rate breaks

I solve it exactly with the derivative, then again with gradient descent, and show why the learning rate has to stay under 2 divided by the curvature or training blows up.
